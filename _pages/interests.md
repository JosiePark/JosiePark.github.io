Sports
======

I compete in Ironman triathlons, having completed my first in Copenhagen in 2023 and my second in Nice in 2025.

I'm a former competitive swimmer, representing my county at school level. During university, I swam with both UCL and Imperial College clubs, serving as President (2014–15) and Treasurer (2013–14) of the UCL Swimming Club.

I now train with the Masters Squad at Cambridge Swimming Club and have transitioned from pool swimming to long-distance open water events. Highlights include completing the 14km Henley Thames Marathon in under 3 hours 30 minutes in 2019, and the shortened 10km Henley Thames Marathon in 2021 in under 2 hours, where I finished 6th female and 16th overall. My personal best for 3km is 46:23.0.
